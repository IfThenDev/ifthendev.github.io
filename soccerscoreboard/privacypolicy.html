Privacy Policy

Soccer Scoreboard

Soccer Scoreboard is built to keep your data on your device. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected — nothing you enter leaves your phone.

Information We Don't Collect

Soccer Scoreboard does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to If Then Dev servers. There is no account and no login. We do not run analytics, we do not track you, and we show no advertising. We do not build profiles or share data with anyone.

Where Your Matches Live

Your matches — team names, scores, the match clock, and the goal-by-goal timeline — are stored on your device using SwiftData, in an App Group container shared with the app's widgets and Live Activity. This data stays on your device and under your control; you can delete individual matches or your entire history at any time from within the app. Nothing is uploaded to If Then Dev.

Purchases

Soccer Scoreboard offers a single one-time $4.99 Pro unlock (Apple Watch, Live Activity, and widgets). Purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit; If Then Dev never sees your payment information, and there is no third-party billing or analytics service involved. Restoring a purchase and confirming prior ownership are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID.

Children's Privacy

Soccer Scoreboard is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone — no names, contacts, accounts, or profiles — and nothing it stores ever leaves the device, so there is nothing to collect from a child. The app is intended to be used by coaches, parents, referees, and fans keeping score.
