Privacy Policy

Bookshelf Scanner

Bookshelf Scanner is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data and never sell your information.

Information We Don't Collect

Bookshelf Scanner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to If Then Dev servers. Apart from the purchase information described under In-App Purchase, we don't use analytics or advertising frameworks, build profiles, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or share data with advertisers.

Camera Access

Bookshelf Scanner uses your device's camera to scan book barcodes and, if you choose Take Photo, to photograph a book's cover. Camera access is requested the first time you use one of these features. Barcode scanning happens in real time on your device; no images or video from scanning are stored or transmitted. A cover photo you take, or one you choose from your photo library, is saved with that book in your library and is stored as described under Local Data Storage and iCloud Sync below. Cover photos are never sent to If Then Dev or any third party.

Book Lookups

When you scan or enter a book's ISBN, Bookshelf Scanner queries third-party book information services to retrieve the book's title, author, publisher, and cover image. These queries include only:

  • The book's ISBN number

No personal information, device identifiers, or user data is sent with these requests. The third-party services used are Google Books, Open Library, and ISBNdb. They receive only the ISBN necessary to return book metadata.

Local Data Storage

Your library data is stored on your device, in Bookshelf Scanner's own private storage, which no other app can read. If iCloud is on, it is also kept in your private iCloud database (see iCloud Sync below). This includes:

  • Book details: titles, authors, ISBNs, cover images, statuses, locations, notes, and dates
  • Student information: first name, last name, borrower code, and optionally email and phone number
  • Checkout history: which student borrowed which book, checkout dates, due dates, and return dates
  • Custom statuses, locations, and app settings

This data remains under your control. You can delete individual books, students, checkout records, or your entire library at any time from within the app, except for the safety copies described below.

After you update to Bookshelf Scanner 3.1, the app keeps a safety copy of your library from before the update in its private storage on your device. If the app ever can't open your library and you choose Start Fresh or Restore, it also keeps the library it couldn't open as a safety copy. Safety copies are never synced to iCloud and are never sent to If Then Dev or anyone else, but like the rest of the app's data they are included in your device's backups. On iPhone and iPad they are removed if you delete the app, and a later version of the app removes them.

Student Data

Bookshelf Scanner allows teachers to enter student names and optional contact information to manage book checkouts. This student data:

  • Is entered and managed entirely by the teacher
  • Is stored only in Bookshelf Scanner's private storage on the device and, if iCloud is on, in the teacher's private iCloud database
  • Is never transmitted to If Then Dev or any third party
  • Is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than library management
  • Can be deleted at any time by the teacher, except for the safety copies described under Local Data Storage, which a later version of the app removes

Teachers are responsible for ensuring their use of student information within Bookshelf Scanner complies with their school's data policies and applicable laws such as FERPA. If Then Dev does not have access to any student data entered into the app.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, your library data (including books, students, and checkout history) may sync across your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account. This sync uses Apple's CloudKit service and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Your data is stored in your private iCloud database and is not accessible to If Then Dev.

Working with QuickScan

If QuickScan is installed on the same device, you can ask it to find reading levels for your books. When you do, Bookshelf Scanner shares each book's ISBN, title, and author with the QuickScan app through a shared container on your device, and QuickScan returns the reading levels it finds. Bookshelf Scanner also reads from that container whether QuickScan's leveling is unlocked. If it is, Bookshelf Scanner automatically reads the reading levels from QuickScan's scan history on your device (ISBNs and reading levels only) and adds them to matching books in your library. No student information is shared with QuickScan, and nothing passes through If Then Dev servers.

In-App Purchase

Bookshelf Scanner offers an optional in-app purchase managed through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage your purchase. RevenueCat receives your purchase history, an anonymous app user ID, a device identifier (not the advertising identifier), and a record of when the purchase screen is shown or closed. It uses this to confirm your purchase and to give us overall purchase statistics. This information is not linked to your name, your email address, or any student data, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details. Your payment information is handled entirely by Apple and is never shared with If Then Dev.

Data Export & Backup

You can export your book and student data as CSV or PDF files, and create full library backups. Exported files and backups are shared only through methods you explicitly choose (Files, AirDrop, etc.). Backups are stored in your iCloud Drive or local device storage and do not pass through If Then Dev servers.

Student File Import

Bookshelf Scanner allows you to import student rosters from CSV or text files stored on your device. Imported files are read locally and are not uploaded or transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy

Bookshelf Scanner is designed for use by teachers to manage classroom libraries. The app does not collect any personal information directly from children. Student information is entered solely by teachers acting in their professional capacity. Children should use Bookshelf Scanner under adult supervision.
